# Rummy Tracker — colorful logo exploration

Five exploratory logo options generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the current logo as a visual reference. The app is an offline scorekeeper for physical Rummy/Remi games.

These are review concepts. Launcher exports and app integration follow the user's choice.

Reference: ../../../rummy_tracker/assets/images/logo_transparent.png

## 1 · Vibrant Remix

[Open PNG](01-vibrant-remix.png)

### Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one original redesigned logo for "Rummy Tracker". This is an offline mobile companion for keeping scores, rounds and all-time wins while friends play physical Rummy/Remi cards.
Input image 1 is the user's CURRENT logo, a visual reference for its energetic colorful personality and prominent full-name lettering. It is NOT an edit target: create a fresh design.
The user disliked minimal, muted, icon-only designs. This round MUST be vivid, expressive, colorful and typographic, closer in spirit to the supplied current logo. Show the full name, exactly "Rummy Tracker", with large exceptionally readable lettering. Use an expressive fan or cluster of playing cards and suit symbols as part of one cohesive compact logo. A scorekeeping cue may be subtly integrated, but do not make it look like a document or checklist app.
Deliver exactly ONE standalone nearly square logo, centered, with generous clear outer margin, on a genuinely transparent background. No contact sheet, explanatory labels, phone mockups, scene, surrounding white or black rectangle, watermark or extra text. Keep the whole silhouette and all lettering inside the image. This is a logo exploration; launcher variants will be prepared after the user chooses a direction.
Direction: a confident high-quality evolution of the original. Hand-drawn slanted sports-style brush lettering, "Rummy" in vivid cherry red and hot pink with cream highlights, "Tracker" in saturated golden yellow and orange. Strong midnight navy keyline ties together the lettering and a cleaner compact ribbon silhouette. Behind the words, four beautifully rendered white playing cards fan outward, alternating red and navy suit symbols. Add vivid turquoise swooshes, two lime-green accents and just a few colorful suit-shaped confetti marks. Rich lively spot colors, crisp vector-like illustration, bold comic ink contours, subtle shiny highlight accents. Match the original's fun and visual richness, with more intentional spacing and much stronger letter legibility. Keep the composition clean enough to recognize as a compact badge. Replace the original fruit decorations with card-suit shapes.
