Privacy policy Play listing text

On the device

Names, scores, rankings, and the current game stay in private storage on the phone.

No wagering

Preset scores are -40, -140, and -500. The lowest total wins. No money moves.

Two languages

The Android app can be used in English or Bosnian, and you can switch at any time.
